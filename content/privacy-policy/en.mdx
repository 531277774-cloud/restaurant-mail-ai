# Privacy Policy

Restaurant Mail AI is a free AI email copywriting tool for restaurant owners. This policy explains what data we handle and your rights under the GDPR, CCPA, and CAN-SPAM.

## 1. What We Collect

- **Usage data**: page views and basic analytics (referrer, UTM parameters, landing page) stored locally in your browser or via third-party analytics such as Google Analytics 4 and pvuv.ai.
- **Content you submit**: restaurant details and email requests you type to generate copy. This content is processed only to produce your output and is not used to identify you personally.

We do **not** require accounts, and we do **not** collect names, physical addresses, or payment information.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To generate and improve email copy.
- To understand traffic and improve the product.

## 3. Email & CAN-SPAM Compliance

Any commercial email you generate and send to your own customers must include your real business mailing address and a working unsubscribe link. The tool inserts placeholders for these fields; you are responsible for replacing them before sending.

## 4. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 and pvuv.ai to measure page views and unique visitors. These services may set cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

- **Access & deletion**: you may request access to or deletion of your personal data.
- **Opt-out of sale**: we do not sell personal data.
